Privacy Policy

BALL FITE Video Uploader is a private desktop application operated by its developer. It uses Google OAuth 2.0 and YouTube API Services to upload original gameplay videos and manage their metadata on an explicitly authorized YouTube channel.

Information accessed

The application accesses only the YouTube account information needed to identify the authorized channel, list that channel's playlists, retrieve assignable video categories, upload videos, and optionally add an uploaded video to a selected playlist. It does not access viewer information, comments, analytics, subscriptions, passwords or unrelated Google account data.

Local storage and retention

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's device and are used only for operator-requested actions. A local upload ledger stores file fingerprints, local file paths, upload timestamps, video titles and returned YouTube video IDs to prevent duplicate uploads. This information remains on the operator's device until the corresponding local files are deleted.

Sharing and advertising

YouTube API data and Google user data are not sold, rented, shared with third parties, transferred for advertising, or used to create advertising profiles. The application has no advertising and does not provide YouTube data to data brokers.

Revoking access and deleting data

The operator may revoke the application's Google access at any time from Google Account third-party connections. Local authorization data can be deleted by removing data/youtube_token.json. Local upload history can be deleted by removing data/video_uploads.sqlite3. Revocation stops future API access but does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; those can be managed in YouTube Studio.

YouTube and Google policies

Use of YouTube information is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes
