Privacy Policy

BALL FITE Video Uploader is a private, single-user desktop application operated by its developer. It uses platform authorization and publishing APIs to send original BALL FITE gameplay videos to accounts explicitly authorized by the operator.

Information accessed

For YouTube, the application accesses the channel identity, playlists and video categories needed to configure and upload videos. For Instagram, it accesses the authorized professional account's identifier, username, display name and account type, and uses the content-publishing permission to prepare or publish operator-selected Reels. For TikTok, the planned integration uses basic profile information to display the authorized destination and the video-upload permission to transfer operator-selected videos into TikTok's inbox editing flow. The application does not request messages, comments, contacts, advertising data, passwords or unrelated account data.

Local storage and retention

Platform access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's device and are used only for operator-requested actions. A local upload ledger stores platform names, file fingerprints, local file paths, upload timestamps, video titles and returned platform media IDs to prevent duplicate uploads. This information remains on the operator's device until the corresponding local files are deleted.

Temporary media transfer

Instagram requires a publicly reachable HTTPS video URL while it ingests a Reel. When temporary hosting is configured, the application uploads only the operator-selected video to a dedicated temporary object-storage location, supplies its unguessable URL to Instagram, waits for ingestion to finish, and then deletes the temporary object. The storage provider processes the file solely to perform this transfer. The application does not use temporarily hosted media for advertising, analytics or unrelated distribution.

Sharing and advertising

Google, YouTube, Meta, Instagram and TikTok account data are not sold, rented, provided to data brokers, transferred for advertising or used to create advertising profiles. Data is sent only to the platform selected by the operator and, when required for Instagram ingestion, the configured temporary storage provider. The application has no advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked from Google Account third-party connections. Instagram access can be removed under Instagram Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites. TikTok access can be removed under TikTok Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. Local authorization data can be deleted by removing the applicable token files under data/, and local upload history can be deleted by removing data/video_uploads.sqlite3. Revocation stops future API access but does not delete previously published media; that media must be managed on the relevant platform.

Platform policies

Platform use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, Meta Privacy Policy, and TikTok Privacy Policy. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
