PRIVATE DESKTOP UTILITY

BALL FITE
Video Uploader

A small, single-user tool that uploads original BALL FITE gameplay recordings to the operator's authorized YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Read the privacy policy Terms of service

PURPOSE

Less repetitive upload work.

The uploader matches locally generated recordings with platform-specific titles, descriptions, tags and publishing settings. Every transfer is operator-confirmed, and accepted uploads are recorded locally to prevent duplicates.

01

Explicit authorization

The operator connects only accounts they own through the platforms' authorization flows and can revoke access at any time.

02

Private by default

YouTube uploads begin as private videos, Instagram offers a no-post ingestion test, and TikTok uses its inbox editing flow.

03

Local data only

Authorization credentials and upload history remain on the operator's computer and are not sold or used for advertising.

▶

PLATFORM API SERVICES

Limited, operator-directed access

The application uses platform APIs only to identify authorized destinations, show relevant posting choices, and transfer operator-selected original videos with their generated metadata.
